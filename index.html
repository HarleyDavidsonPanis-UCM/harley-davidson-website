03 / The Legend

120+ years of thunder

The milestones that made me fall in love with the brand.

  1. 1903

    A shed in Milwaukee

    William Harley and Arthur Davidson build their first motorcycle in a small wooden shed.

  2. 1909

    The first V-twin

    The V-twin layout arrives. That V-twin rumble is still the soul of the brand.

  3. 1936

    The Knucklehead

    The EL model brings an overhead-valve engine and a design that became an icon.

  4. 1957

    Enter the Sportster

    A lighter, quicker bike that gave riders a sportier way to cruise.

  5. 1965

    Electra Glide

    Electric start makes long-distance touring easier and more comfortable.

  6. 1984

    The Evolution engine

    A big step forward in reliability, helping the brand win over a new generation.

  7. 2017

    Milwaukee-Eight

    A modern engine for touring and Softail models, still with that V-twin character.
